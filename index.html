FIELD NOTES B.TECH ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

From a tiny circuit
to a living grid.

Electrical engineering connects the fundamentals of circuits to the systems that move, compute, and power our world. These are some of the areas I’m excited to explore during my degree.

01 / THE FUNDAMENTALS ⏚

Circuits & electrical machines

Build intuition for voltage, current, and circuits, then discover how transformers and electric machines convert energy.

Circuit analysis · Machines · Measurements
02 / THE GRID ⌁

Power & renewable energy

Explore how electricity is generated, transmitted, and delivered—and how solar and wind can become part of a reliable grid.

Power systems · Solar · Wind integration
03 / THE CONTROL ◉

Electronics & control

Learn how power electronics shape electrical energy and how control systems help machines respond with precision.

Power electronics · Control · EVs
04 / THE TOOLKIT ⌘

Code, signals & simulation

Use programming and simulation to model ideas, understand signals, and test engineering concepts before they reach hardware.

Python · MATLAB · Signals & systems

✳ These are learning interests—not a claim of expertise. I’m looking forward to building experience throughout my degree.
